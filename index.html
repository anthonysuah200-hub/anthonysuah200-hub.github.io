LESSON 1

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and information from unauthorized access, misuse, damage or disruption.

Common Cyber Threats

  • Phishing: deceptive messages designed to trick people.
  • Malware: harmful software that can compromise systems.
  • Password attacks: attempts to steal or guess passwords.
  • Social engineering: manipulating people into revealing information.
⚠️ Security Rule

Never test, access or attack a computer system without permission. Practice cybersecurity only in authorized environments.

LESSON 2

Protect Your Accounts

Your online accounts contain valuable personal information. Strong authentication and good security habits can reduce the risk of account compromise.

Good Security Habits

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Be careful with unexpected links and attachments.

LESSON 3

Network Security Basics

Networks allow computers and devices to communicate. Network security focuses on protecting those connections, devices and information moving through them.

Important Concepts

  • Router: directs traffic between networks.
  • Firewall: helps control network traffic.
  • Encryption: protects information by transforming it into a protected form.
  • Wi-Fi security: helps protect wireless networks from unauthorized access.
