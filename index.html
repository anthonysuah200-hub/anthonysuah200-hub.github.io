LESSON 1 • BEGINNER

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, phones, networks, applications and digital information from unauthorized access, attacks, damage or theft.

🎯 What You'll Learn

  • What cybersecurity means
  • Why cybersecurity is important
  • Common types of cyber threats
  • How attackers target people and organizations
  • Basic ways to protect yourself

🛡️ Why Does Cybersecurity Matter?

Almost everything we do today involves technology. We use phones and computers for communication, banking, education, business and entertainment.

If these systems are not protected, attackers may steal information, take over accounts, spread malware or disrupt important services.

💡 Smart Tip: Cybersecurity is not only about protecting computers. It is also about protecting people, information and digital identities.

⚠️ Common Cyber Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake messages or websites designed to trick people into giving away information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software designed to damage systems, steal information or gain unauthorized access.

🔑 Password Attacks

Attempts to discover or steal passwords and gain access to accounts.

👤 Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or performing an unsafe action.

🌍 Real-World Example

Imagine receiving an email saying: "Your bank account will be closed today. Click here to verify."

The message may look legitimate, but the link could lead to a fake website designed to steal your login information.

🚨 Remember: Never enter your password or sensitive information on a website simply because a message tells you to.
