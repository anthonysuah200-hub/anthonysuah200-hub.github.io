🛡️ Learn Ethically. Practice Responsibly.

Smart Tech Hub promotes responsible cybersecurity education. Only assess systems you own or have explicit permission to test. Use controlled labs for practical exercises, respect privacy, and never use your skills to access other people's accounts or systems without authorization.
