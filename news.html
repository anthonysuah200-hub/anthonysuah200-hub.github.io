🛡️ Smart Security Reminder

  • Use strong, unique passwords for important accounts.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication whenever possible.
  • Never share passwords, verification codes or recovery codes.
  • Think before clicking links in unexpected messages.
  • Keep your operating system and applications updated.
  • Practice cybersecurity skills only in systems you are authorized to test.
